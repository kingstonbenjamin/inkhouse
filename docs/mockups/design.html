Inkhouse, design mockup

The interactive design mockup used before the real panel was built: a Kindle Paperwhite drawn to scale, grayscale only, touch targets of at least 10 mm. The first screen is live (tap tiles, sheets and the bottom bar); the others are frozen states. All values are sample data. For the real page running on sample data, see the live demo.

Borrowed from popular HA dashboards

  • Mushroom: the round icon switches the device, the rest of the card opens details.
  • Bubble Card: bottom navigation and pop-up sheets.
  • Minimalist UI: rounded cards with one short state line.

Made for e-ink

  • Black, paper and one gray; "on" is a filled circle and a thicker border.
  • No animation, so no ghosting; one redraw per change.
  • Risky actions (all AC off, unlock) ask first.
  • Idle clock after 2 minutes, night mode for the bedroom.

Usual settings

  • The vacuum and dishwasher sheets underline the settings you normally use, so one tap starts your usual run.
  • In the real panel, the "usual" values come from the config file.
